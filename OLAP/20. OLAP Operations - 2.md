## 🚀 20. OLAP Operations - 2

### 📌 Problem

This OLAP operation rotates the data and delivers an alternative to the original presentation.

- Pivot
- Slice
- Roll-up
- Dice

---

### 💡 Explanation

The **Pivot** operation rotates the multidimensional view of data by reorienting its dimensions. This provides an alternative perspective of the same data, making it easier to analyze relationships from different viewpoints.

For example, a report displaying **Sales by Product and Region** can be pivoted to show **Sales by Region and Product** without changing the underlying data.

---

### 💻 Solution

```
Pivot
```
