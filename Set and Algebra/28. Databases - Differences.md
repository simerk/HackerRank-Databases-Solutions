## 🚀 28. Databases - Differences

### 📌 Problem

Relation **R(A, B, C)** has the following tuples:

| A | B | C |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 | 3 |
| 4 | 5 | 6 |
| 2 | 5 | 3 |
| 1 | 2 | 6 |

Relation **S(A, B, C)** has the following tuples:

| A | B | C |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 5 | 3 |
| 2 | 5 | 4 |
| 4 | 5 | 6 |
| 1 | 2 | 3 |

The difference **R − S** is computed, and the following tuple is present in the result:

```
(4, b, c)
```

Find the values of **b** and **c**.

---

### 💡 Explanation

The **set difference** operation (`R − S`) returns all tuples that are present in **R** but **not** in **S**.

Comparing the tuples:

| Tuple | In R | In S | In R − S |
|-------|:----:|:----:|:--------:|
| (1, 2, 3) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| (4, 2, 3) | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| (4, 5, 6) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| (2, 5, 3) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| (1, 2, 6) | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |

The tuple beginning with `A = 4` in **R − S** is:

```
(4, 2, 3)
```

Therefore:

- `b = 2`
- `c = 3`

---

### 💻 Solution

```text
2
3
```
